Beyon — Phase 141 → 150
Scale, Performance, Search, Realtime & Platform Intelligence

Execution rule: Implement one phase at a time. Before starting, inspect the existing codebase and reuse existing architecture. Do not replace working implementations just to introduce a new technology. These phases focus on application-level scaling, not unnecessary DevOps infrastructure.

   ↓
