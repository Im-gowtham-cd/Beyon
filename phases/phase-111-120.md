Most important separation

Keep these three systems independent:

Beyon Coins
    ↓
Used for company opportunity access


Skill XP
    ↓
Measures learning progression


Growth Score
    ↓
Measures overall career readiness

This prevents the gamification system from becoming exploitable and keeps Beyon Coins as the actual opportunity-access currency.
