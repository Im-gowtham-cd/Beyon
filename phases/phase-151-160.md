Important implementation rule

Do not make Beyon dependent entirely on an LLM.

Use deterministic Beyon logic for:

Eligibility
Coin calculations
Skill scores
Assessment results
Company requirements
Placement status
Certification verification

Use AI primarily for:

Explanation
Personalization
Recommendations
Career guidance
Resume/portfolio assistance
Natural-language interaction

That keeps Beyon's most important decisions accurate, explainable, and controllable.
